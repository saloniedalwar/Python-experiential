About this project

Mad Libs Generator is a story app. It uses your words to create a short story, and every story ends with a moral.

  • Six words go in: name, adjective, goal, friend, action, animal.
  • Three story templates, or let the app pick one at random.
  • Empty fields are flagged before a story is written.
